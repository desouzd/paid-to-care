Recovery coaches: certification, the new license, and pay

Massachusetts passed a law (Chapter 285 of the Acts of 2024, signed December 23, 2024) that creates a state license for recovery coaches and requires insurers to cover recovery coaching.

Part 1

Start here: steps only you can do

Recovery coaching is a peer role, so these depend on your own experience and training.

  1. The CCAR Recovery Coach Academy is the standard starting training in Massachusetts and is offered by several organizations around the state.
    Find a training →
  2. The Certified Addiction Recovery Coach (CARC) credential from the Massachusetts Board of Substance Abuse Counselor Certification (MBSACC) expects:
    • Personal experience with addiction and recovery, usually at least 1 year of sustained recovery (MassHealth's peer recovery coach role requires 2 years)
    • A high school diploma or GED
    • 60 hours of in-person education across advocacy, mentoring, recovery/wellness support and ethics (online courses don't count for first-time certification)
    • 500 hours of supervised work, paid or volunteer
    • Living or working in Massachusetts at least 51% of the time
    See the full requirements →
Part 2

Next steps: paperwork and finding work

Take it one step at a time. Each step links to the official source.

  1. The application, including the supervisor form, must be hand-written by you. Typed applications are returned. Make copies of everything, including your training certificates and your supervisor's sign-off, before you mail it.
  2. The 2024 law (Chapter 285) moves recovery coach licensing to the Department of Public Health. Once DPH's process is live, you'll likely need the license to use the "recovery coach" title and be paid by insurers.
    Read the new law →
  3. Most recovery coaches are paid as employees of treatment programs, hospitals, community health centers and recovery community centers. MassHealth covers recovery coaching, and private insurers must cover it for plans starting or renewing on or after January 1, 2026, so more of these employers can afford to hire coaches.
    Browse recovery jobs →

The licensing and insurance rules are new and still being put in place. Confirm details with DPH, MBSACC and your employer.
