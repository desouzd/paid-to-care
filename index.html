The problem

Good news, with a catch.

MassHealth started paying doulas in December 2023, up to about $1,700 per client. But the money only comes after you get through the enrollment paperwork, and nobody really teaches you how.

You have to request the forms, complete a training on a confusing website, gather proof or recommendation letters, and mail it all in. Applications take about 60 days, and one missing page can send you back to the start. Until you're approved, you can't be paid for the work you're already doing.

Our mission

The people who show up for others should get paid for it, without drowning in paperwork.

We turn government and insurance rules into plain-English steps, so the forms, deadlines and follow-up don't get in the way of the care you give.

About

Who's behind Paid to Care MA

Paid to Care MA was started by Derek deSouza, a pre-med student at Boston College who saw how much paperwork stands between doulas, recovery coaches and getting paid for their work.

We're independent and focused on Massachusetts. The guides are free and built only from official state sources, and we're always looking for feedback from doulas and recovery coaches about what's confusing or missing.

Questions, feedback, or stuck on a step?
