The problem

Good news with a catch.

The state recently started paying doulas, and new laws require insurance to cover recovery coaches. But the money only comes after you get through the paperwork, and nobody teaches you how.

Confusing formsGovernment applications, ID numbers and sworn statements that have to be done in the right order.
Long waits, no answersApplications sit for weeks, and one missing page sends you back to the start.
Money left on the tableUntil you're approved, you can't be paid for the families and people you're already helping.

Our mission

The people who show up for others should get paid for it, without drowning in paperwork.

We turn confusing government and insurance rules into simple steps in plain English. You focus on your clients; we make sure the forms, deadlines and follow-ups don't stand in the way.
