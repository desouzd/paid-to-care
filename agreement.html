Free help agreement

Plain-language terms for free MassHealth enrollment help. You agree to these when you submit the free-help questionnaire.

1. What this is

Derek deSouza ("we") will help you apply to become a MassHealth doula provider, free of charge. This help covers only your MassHealth enrollment application.

2. What we do

  • Fill out your official MassHealth application forms using the answers you give in the questionnaire.
  • Request recommendation letters for you, if you use the experience pathway.
  • Follow up with MassHealth about the status of your application (see section 4).

3. What you do

  • Get your own NPI number and complete the MassHealth Doula Provider Training yourself.
  • Request your own application package from MassHealth and forward it to us.
  • Review every form before signing. You are responsible for the accuracy of your application.
  • Fill in your own Social Security number, tax ID and bank details, sign every form, and mail the package to MassHealth yourself. We will never ask for or keep this information.

4. Permission to talk to MassHealth

You allow us to be listed as a contact on your MassHealth enrollment application, and to communicate with MassHealth and its contractor (Maximus) only about the status and completeness of your enrollment application. This permission:

  • does not cover billing, claims, payments or any information about your clients;
  • does not allow us to sign anything or make changes for you;
  • ends when your application is approved or denied, or anytime you tell us to stop (an email or text is enough).

5. No guarantee

Only MassHealth decides whether to approve your application. We can't guarantee approval or timing.

6. Privacy

We keep your questionnaire answers and application documents only while helping you, store them privately, never share them with anyone except MassHealth, and delete them within 30 days after MassHealth decides on your application.

7. Feedback

In return for free help, we'll ask for your honest feedback about the process. We'll only share a testimonial with your permission.

Version October 7, 2026. Plain-language agreement for a free pilot program; not legal advice. Questions: paidtocarema@gmail.com.
