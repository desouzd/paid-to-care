For families

Looking for a doula?

If you have MassHealth, a doula may be covered at no cost to you. Here's what's covered and how to find a MassHealth doula near you.

The basics

What a doula does

A doula gives you and your family non-medical support during pregnancy, birth and after your baby arrives: answering questions, helping you prepare for birth, comfort and encouragement during labor, and checking in afterward. A doula doesn't replace your doctor or midwife. They work alongside your care team.

Coverage

What MassHealth covers

MassHealth covers doula services from doulas who are enrolled as MassHealth providers:

  • Support during labor and birth, including support during a miscarriage or abortion.
  • Up to 8 hours of visits, which you and your doula can use any time during pregnancy and up to 12 months after.
  • More visits if needed: your doula can ask MassHealth to approve extra hours.
  • Coverage also includes adoptive parents of a baby under one year old.
You should never pay out of pocket

MassHealth says members should never pay out of pocket for MassHealth-covered doula services. Make sure the doula you choose is a MassHealth doula provider.

How to find one

Find a MassHealth doula near you

  1. Open the MassHealth Provider Directory.
  2. Click Specialists, then choose Doula from the specialty menu.
  3. Look for doulas near you, and check the languages they speak.
  4. Reach out to a few. Tell them you have MassHealth and ask them to confirm they're a MassHealth doula provider. It's okay to talk with more than one before you choose.
